RIYA'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE: HOW IT WORKS
=====================================

Open index.html. The secret code is 2007. Host the whole folder on GitHub Pages (or Netlify) to share a link.

FILES
  index.html, birthday.html, door.html ...   one file per page (the order is the FLOW list in common.js)
  common.css      colours, fonts, buttons and the background for every theme
  common.js       shared engine: page changes, auto-advance bar, handwriting pen, roses, keypad, helpers
  motion.js       extra polish: tap sparkles, next-page prefetch, keyboard help, "never stuck" helpers
  config.js       EVERYTHING you may want to change: names, dates, photo names, captions, questions, texts
  photos/         her photos (her1.jpg ...), together.jpg, and the artwork made for you (see UPLOAD-THESE.txt)
  videos/         her four videos and the music tape video (see README.txt inside)
  check.html      shows which photos, videos and artwork were found

THINGS TO SET IN config.js
  from            your name
  whatsapp        your WhatsApp number with country code, digits only (for example 919876543210)
  friendsSince    2024-06-15
  photos / polaroids   the compliment written under each picture
  videos          the title and words beside each of her videos
  qa              the questions she answers on the "Your turn" page
  Any photo path may also be a full web link (https://...), so photos can come from other web pages.

THE ORDER OF THE JOURNEY
  common.js -> FLOW. Move a name in that list to move a page. Pages that are not in the list
  (cinema, postcard, garden, notebook, tape) open from the gift box and have a Back button.

HOW THE PAGES MOVE ON BY THEMSELVES
  A thin bar at the bottom fills and the next page opens. The round button pauses it, and it also
  pauses when she leaves the browser tab. Pages that need a tap (door, band, sealed letters, gift box)
  play the next step by themselves if she does nothing for about 15 seconds. Locks (secret code,
  passkey) always wait for her.

HOW HER ANSWERS REACH YOU
  A website cannot send a WhatsApp message on its own. When she finishes, the button builds ONE picture
  with every question, answer and photo, then opens the phone's share sheet (she picks WhatsApp and your
  chat). On a computer the picture is saved, the text is copied and WhatsApp opens with the text ready.
  config.js -> answersEndpoint can also post the text to a form service (Formspree, Web3Forms) so you
  get a copy by email.

TIPS
  Keep each photo under about 1.5 MB and each video under 50 MB so pages load quickly.
  The finished zip is small (about 2.5 MB); your photos and videos are added to the folders separately.
