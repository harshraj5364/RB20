HOW THIS WORKS
Put the files in this photos folder using EXACTLY these names.
Anything you do not upload keeps a simple drawn or emoji stand-in, so the site always works.
Open check.html (in the main folder) to see which files were found (green tick) and which are missing.
"Cut-out" means a PNG with a transparent background (about 800 to 1200 px on the long side).
